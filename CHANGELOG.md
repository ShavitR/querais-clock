# Changelog

## 1.1.0 — First community release

- Four original 3D clocks: Atelier, Slate, Relay and Signal.
- Flat analog and digital alternatives.
- Crown, orbit, caseback, inspection, glass, turntable and studio lights.

- Editable Blender source, GLBs and original artwork.
